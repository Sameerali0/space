# Astro Star Collecter

Astro Star Collecter is a simple game where you play as astronaut trying to survive by dodging rocks and fast rockets, and collects stars for increase your points each star gives +1 score and every 10 points increases your level and also increases the speed, making the game gets harder. the player have 3 lives and if you get hit by rocks 3 times the game is over if you get hit by a rocket even once the game is over.

## How to Play
- ⬆ ⬇ ⬅ ➡ Move your astronaut
- Collect stars to increase score
- Avoid rocks they reduce lives
- Avoid rockets instant game over!
